Eagle 38

Model 38 is an expression of modern elegance. The underwater line implements a number of innovative design solutions. This boat is a full-size daysailer and it can comfortably accommodate up to six people in the cockpit. For the ease of use, the jib capstans have been located within reach of the helmsman. The halyard winch has been placed on the superstructure and connected to the roller system of the jib mounted below the deck.

The interior of the yacht is finished with lacquered mahogany and the ceiling is covered with Alcantara. The warm light of the led lamps, combined with large amounts of sunlight, create an ambience of elegance and luxury. The interior has space for three people to sleep comfortably and the toilet is convenient and neat. Optionally, the yacht may be equipped with a sink and a stove.
