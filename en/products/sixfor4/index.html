SiXfor4

SiXfor4 is an elegant small cruiser with a modern design. Its wide waterline and the low center of gravity render SiXfor4 as a particularly safe and stable yacht. It is also a great choice for lone sailors. Due to its low static weight and its relatively minute dimensions, this unit can be easily transported on a trailer. There is comfortable cockpit-room for four people and also remarkably much space and comfort under the deck (which incudes berths for four people).
