Eagle 44

The Eagle 44 Model is a typical daysailer. It was designed in the Netherlands and it has a classic look, yet it is constructed from modern materials. Its steering wheel has been made from mahogany and stainless steel which ensure an excellent steering experience underpinned by remarkable maneuverability. For the ease of use, the jib capstans have been located within reach of the helmsman. The teak seats, covered with high-end upholstery, can accommodate up to eight people. The interior of the yachts has been made from mahogany. The ceiling is covered with high-quality Alcantara.
